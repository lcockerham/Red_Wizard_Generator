# 2024 rules audit

What each rule and stat-block convention in the generator is based on today, what the 2024 rules (SRD 5.2.1 / 2024 *Monster Manual*) do instead, and what would have to change. This is a survey only: nothing in the code changes here.

Audited at `a310f48` (main, after PR #5), 2026-11-19.

## Short answer

The generator is a hybrid. The **Actions block already copies the 2024 Mage** (Multiattack plus Arcane Burst, and a Spellcasting action with At Will / 2/Day Each / 1/Day Each lists). **The numbers come from 2014 player-character rules**: proficiency bonus, saves, skills and spell DC all scale with a 1–20 character level, and HP is a per-level formula. The other wording (labels, Saving Throws line, Challenge line) is **2014 *Monster Manual* layout**. The 2024 version of a monster has no character level. Everything scales from Challenge Rating (CR), so most of the conversion follows from one decision: how a generated wizard gets a CR (row 1).

## Sources and how they're cited

- **SRD 5.2.1** is cited as `SRD 5.2.1 p.N, LA–B`. The page is the PDF page, which matches the SRD's own table of contents. `L` is the line in `SRD_CC_v5.2.1.layout.txt` (`pdftotext -layout` of the CC-BY PDF from D&D Beyond). That is the same file and line numbering as `dnd-bench/srd/citations.md`, and I spot-checked lines 312, 1402–1406 and 16616–16630. Neither checkout has the SRD text or dnd-llm's ChromaDB, so I downloaded the public PDF to read it. Nothing from it is committed. Note: dnd-bench's page numbers run one lower than the PDF pages (for example, it puts the spell DC formula on p.22, but it's on p.23). Its line numbers are correct.
- **SRD 5.1** (the 2014 SRD, same source) is cited by page, and only to pin down the 2014 Mage and Archmage.
- **dnd-llm** is cited by path: `library/monsters-2024.md` (2024 MM index) and `.claude/reference/house-style.md`.
- SRD 5.2.1 has **no stat-by-CR targets** (AC, HP, damage per round), as `dnd-bench/srd/citations.md` ("Not found in this SRD") also notes. The proposed numbers in rows 1, 5, 9 and 11 are therefore interpolated between the SRD's Wizard-tag NPCs. Those anchors are:

## Recommendation

Convert the numeric rows first, because they change what's at the table. Then do the cosmetic rows, then the spell content. Every step should regenerate and render (`python red_wizard_generator.py 3 && python red_wizard_to_html.py`, per CLAUDE.md) and update the tests that pin the old formulas.

1. **CR foundation (rows 1, 2, 3, 7, 15).** Add `cr` and `xp` fields from `level`, pass CR into `calculate_proficiency_bonus`, and print the 2024 CR line. Spell DC, attack bonus, saves and skills then correct themselves. Include the CR-keyed Int schedule, or the DCs run high. This is one small PR and unblocks everything else.
2. **Arcane Burst and Multiattack (row 11).** Apply the damage rule below. This closes `TODO.txt:1`. (`TODO.txt:2`, spell save DC from level and Int, was done earlier and can be removed.)
3. **HP, AC, Initiative (rows 5, 6, 9).** Average-of-dice HP, Mage Armor AC and the Initiative entry. HP and AC change the CR math the most after damage.
4. **Layout and wording pass (rows 4, 8, 10, 12, 13, 20).** One template PR: header with the (Wizard) tag, MOD/SAVE table, Title Case spells, a Reactions section for Shield, and the spell attack bonus in the Spellcasting line.
5. **Spell content (row 14).** CR-keyed tiers and real per-school lists. This is the largest and most taste-driven change, so do it last (`TODO.txt:3`).

Leave as they are: rows 16–19 (Passive Perception, Speed, Languages, Alignment). They already match 2024 or are flavour. The edition-neutral bugs (negative Con in the HP expression, duplicated "Multiattack.", halfling size, undead type, duplicate languages) are each a one-line fix and can ride along with whichever step touches that line.

### Arcane Burst damage rule

Use **Force** damage, **+ Int modifier** on every hit, and dice and attack count by CR. The to-hit stays `PB + Int mod` (already wired since PR #5). The Int modifier matches all three SRD anchors (Mage +3 with Int 17, Archmage +5 with Int 20, Lich +5 with Int 21).

| CR | Multiattack | Arcane Burst damage | Range | Damage per round at Int mod | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | two | 2d6 + Int | 120 ft. | ~20 (+3) | Priest CR 2: two × 11 (2d10); Druid CR 2: two × 10 (3d6) |
| 3–5 | two | 3d8 + Int | 120 ft. | ~33 (+3) | interpolated |
| 6–8 | three | 3d8 + Int | 120 ft. | ~49 (+3) | **Mage CR 6: three × 16 (3d8 + 3)** |
| 9–11 | three | 3d10 + Int | 120 ft. | ~61 (+4) | interpolated (the dice the template uses today) |
| 12–13 | four | 4d10 + Int | 150 ft. | ~108 (+5) | **Archmage CR 12: four × 27 (4d10 + 5)** |

The CR 11 → 12 jump (about 61 → 108) is inherited from the 2024 Archmage. If it plays too hot, use three attacks at CR 12–13 (about 81). The level → CR mapping in row 1 tops out at CR 13, so no higher tier is needed. The Lich (CR 21, three × 4d12 + 5) is the ceiling if the range is ever widened.
